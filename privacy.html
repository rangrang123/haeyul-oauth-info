Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Haeyul YouTube Analytics is operated solely to analyze the performance of the Haeyul Piano YouTube channel.

Data accessed

With the channel owner’s authorization, the tool accesses read-only YouTube and YouTube Analytics data, including video metadata and performance metrics such as impressions, click-through rate, views, watch time, audience retention, and traffic sources.

How data is used

Data is used only to create internal weekly performance reports and, when configured by the owner, to append report summaries to the owner’s Google Sheet.

Data sharing and retention

The tool does not sell, rent, transfer, or share authorized Google user data with third parties. OAuth credentials are stored only as encrypted repository secrets for the owner’s automated workflow. Report artifacts are retained only as configured by the repository owner.

Data controls

The channel owner may revoke this application’s Google account access at any time from the Google Account security settings. Revocation prevents further access.
